Udyam Setu — data privacy and security choices

One-page note for the Airtel SYNC / Project Udyam Digital submission · Accel Business (fictional operator) · Safal Gupta, VIT Vellore · designed against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DoT customer-acquisition (CAF) obligations · all data synthetic. The CAF retention period is set by DoT rules and confirmed with Accel's regulatory team before launch.

1. Principle: look up before you ask, ask before you store

Needs discovery uses no personal data at all: business type, size, city tier and current internet are enough to price a bundle. Identity data enters only at KYC, after an explicit consent screen, and the first move is a registry lookup (GSTIN / Udyam), so the owner uploads a document only when the lookup cannot answer.

2. Consent (DPDP s.5–6): specific, itemised, in the owner's language

  • Notice shown before any upload, in the language the owner chose for the conversation (Hindi, Marathi and English in the prototype). Each purpose is a separate line: identity verification, service activation and the regulatory customer application form, and marketing on WhatsApp. The first two are required to proceed; marketing is unticked by default and can be refused without affecting the order.
  • Each consent goes to an append-only ledger (purposes, time, language, notice version 2026-10-v1). Withdrawal is one tap on the tracker page; marketing stops at once, verification data follows the retention below.

3. Data minimisation and what is never stored

DataTreatment
GSTIN, Udyam numberStored in the KYC store (encrypted at field level, India region). Everywhere else — UI, partner app, logs, analytics — only the masked form (27QH…1ZL) exists.
Documents for an activated customer (the CAF and the supporting copies it must carry)Kept in an encrypted vault for exactly the period DoT subscriber-verification rules require for CAF records, then purged; every read is logged with the user and a reason. Nothing beyond what the CAF needs is kept.
Everything else: rejected or abandoned applications, retakes, OCR intermediatesPurged within 7 days. The prototype deletes images right after extraction.
PAN, Aadhaar number, bank detailsNever stored. OTP-based Aadhaar eSign through a licensed eSign Service Provider returns the signed form; the Aadhaar number does not enter our systems. Payment runs on a UPI autopay mandate token, not account details.
An existing Accel mobile customer's verified identityReused only for the signatory (the individual) and only with fresh consent. The business is always verified separately through its GSTIN/Udyam record, so one person's mobile KYC is never treated as the business's KYC.
Phone numberMasked in all tables except the notification service; used only for OTP, order updates and, if consented, marketing.
Text sent to the language modelPhone, e-mail, GSTIN, PAN, Aadhaar and Udyam numbers redacted first (tested). It returns only values from a fixed list, which are validated; it never sees documents or sets a price.

4. Purpose limitation and sharing

Verification data is used for verification and the CAF only, never for propensity scoring (which uses the non-identifying lead profile). Partners see masked identifiers and the pre-filled quote, never document images. Customer records, documents and the consent ledger are stored only in India. Every processor is named in the notice with its role: GST and Udyam registry APIs and the eSign / DigiLocker providers (verification); Meta's WhatsApp Business platform (messages and phone number, under its business terms); the language-model provider (redacted text only, no identifiers or documents, no training on it; production runs it in an India region or on a model Accel hosts).

5. Security controls

  • Encryption and access: TLS and encryption at rest, field-level for identifiers, keys outside the app; role-based access, one application at a time for verification officers, alerts on bulk reads.
  • Audit: every lookup, consent, decision and status change is written with actor, action, masked subject and reason (visible in the prototype's back office).
  • Fraud resistance: OCR look-alike repair is accepted only when the result passes the GSTIN checksum and exists in the registry; a GSTIN that is cancelled or suspended, a legal-name mismatch, or an address proof older than 90 days is held for a human, never auto-passed. Session OTP, device binding and OTP-bound eSign protect against someone else completing KYC for a business.
  • Accountability: a Data Protection Impact Assessment before launch. An operator of Accel's size is likely to be notified a Significant Data Fiduciary, so a Data Protection Officer in India, independent data audits and periodic DPIAs are built into the plan.
  • Rights (DPDP s.11–14): the owner can view, correct, download or delete their data and withdraw consent from the tracker page; grievance contact and the Data Protection Board route are shown in the notice.
